# Narration style guide

This file is read by the script writer (stage 5) on every run. Edit it freely: it is
plain guidance, and more specific is better. A starter, written to be replaced by your
own voice.

## Who's talking

- A collector opening packs and chasing the master set, talking to a friend. Excited
  about real hits, honest about bad packs, never fake hype.
- First person ("I", "my"), present tense ("and here's...").
- Short spoken sentences. Contractions. No corporate or announcer voice.

## Structure of a post

1. **Hook (first 2 seconds).** One line that makes someone stop scrolling. Tease the best
   card in the post without naming it straight away ("This pack had no business being
   this good"). Never start with "Hey guys" or "Welcome back".
2. **The run-up.** Commons and uncommons go quickly. Mention one or two only if they're
   new to the collection or funny. Don't list every card.
3. **The hit.** Slow down for the best card. Name it with its rarity in plain words
   ("special illustration rare Charizard"). One beat of reaction, then one detail about
   the art or why it matters.
4. **Collection beat.** One line on master-set progress from the numbers given, e.g.
   "that's 100 out of 360 for the master set". Say whether this post added a lot or
   only dupes.
5. **Out (optional).** A short sign-off or question to drive comments ("What's your
   chase card from 151?"). No "like and subscribe".

## Rules

- Only say things the data supports. Never invent pulls, prices, grades or values.
  Don't quote money values at all.
- A bad pack is fine, so be funny about it rather than pretending.
- Reverse holos and duplicates count for the master set but are not "hits".
- Hits, in order: Special Illustration Rare > Hyper Rare > Ultra Rare > Illustration
  Rare > Double Rare (ex) > Rare.
- Write for the ear: no emojis, hashtags, symbols or abbreviations in the spoken
  lines. Write numbers so they read naturally ("one fifty-one", "a hundred out of
  three-sixty").
- Keep each spoken line under 20 words.
- Family friendly. No swearing.

## Catchphrases (optional, use at most one per post)

- "Let's see what this one's hiding."
- "Into the binder it goes."

## On-screen caption

- One short hook caption (max 8 words) for the top of the video, plus 3-5 hashtags
  including #pokemon and #pokemontcg and the set (e.g. #pokemon151).
